# 2-Week Plan — Target: 2–2.5 kg

**Start:** 85 kg → **Day 14 target:** 82.5–83 kg
**Daily intake:** ~1,740 kcal | ~131g protein
**Maintenance:** ~2,500 kcal | **Deficit:** ~760 kcal/day

Note: Week 1 loss will look faster (water + glycogen). Week 2 is the real rate. Don't panic if week 2 shows only 0.6–0.8 kg — that's correct.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **Oil cap: 3 tsp/day total.** Measure it into a spoon, don't pour from the bottle. This is where the plan most commonly fails — 6 tsp instead of 3 is +200 kcal/day and turns 2.5 kg into 1.5 kg.
2. **No ghee on roti.**
3. **Dal tadka: 1 tsp oil. No cream, no malai.**
4. **Paneer raw from packet** — pasteurised/branded only, finish within 2–3 days of opening, keep refrigerated.
5. **Water: 3+ litres/day.**
6. **No sugar in tea/coffee.**

## Movement

**Walking — daily, non-negotiable**
- Morning before office: 30 min (~3 km)
- After dinner: 20 min (~1.5–2 km)
- Total ~5 km/day ≈ 250 kcal

**Bodyweight training — Mon / Wed / Fri, 20 min**

This is what decides whether you end up at 82.5 kg *trim* or 82.5 kg *soft*. Skip it and roughly a third of your loss is muscle.

*Circuit — 3 rounds, 60 sec rest between rounds:*

| Exercise | Reps |
|---|---|
| Push-ups (knee push-ups if needed) | 8–12 |
| Bodyweight squats | 15 |
| Reverse lunges | 10 each leg |
| Plank | 30 sec |
| Glute bridge | 15 |
| Superman hold | 20 sec |

*Progression:* add 2 reps per exercise each week. When push-ups hit 15 easily, slow the lowering to 3 seconds instead of adding reps.

*Warm-up (3 min):* arm circles, leg swings, 20 jumping jacks, 10 bodyweight squats.

## Tracking

- **Weigh:** same time daily — after waking, after toilet, before anything else. Record it, but judge by the **weekly average**, not day-to-day. Daily swings of ±1 kg are water and mean nothing.
- **Waist:** measure at navel, Day 1 and Day 14. If the scale stalls but waist drops, the plan is working.
- **Photos:** front and side, Day 1 and Day 14, same light and time.

## One Thing Not in the Chart

Cigarettes at 8–10/day directly limit how hard you can walk and train — you'll get winded on the 30-minute walk and it'll be tempting to cut it short. Getting to 4–5/day over these two weeks would make everything above meaningfully easier to execute. It won't change the calorie math, but it changes whether you actually finish the workouts.
